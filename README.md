# iKin Asia Summit Championship Season 1

**iKin Asia Summit Championship (iKin ASC)** is a non-ranked online Counter-Strike 2 tournament organized by iKin, featuring qualifiers across China, South China, and Asia, leading to an 8-team double-elimination playoff.

---

## Qualifier Paths

- **China Qualifier** – 256/128 teams → 4 slots, open to all Chinese teams (including South China)
- **South China Qualifier** – 256/128 teams → 2 slots, restricted to teams from Guangdong, Guangxi, Hainan, Fujian, etc.
- **Asia Qualifier** – 256/128 teams → 2 slots, open to all Asian teams (max 2 Chinese players per mixed roster)
