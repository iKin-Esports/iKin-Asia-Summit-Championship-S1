## 1. Tournament Overview
**iKin Asia Summit Championship S1** is a CS2 non-ranked event with three regional qualifiers feeding into an 8-team double-elimination main event.

## 2. Qualifier Paths

| Qualifier | Slots | Eligibility |
|-----------|-------|--------------|
| China Qualifier | 4 | All Chinese teams (including South China) |
| South China Qualifier | 2 | Teams from specified southern provinces only |
| Asia Qualifier | 2 | All Asian teams; max 2 Chinese players per mixed roster |

All qualifiers start with up to 256 teams (may be reduced to 128). Format: Single Elimination, BO1 early rounds, BO3 from Top 16/32.

## 3. Main Event
- 8 teams, Double Elimination bracket.
- Early WB matches BO1, rest BO3, Grand Final BO5 (WB champion gets 1-map advantage).

## 4. Schedule Framework (TBA)
- Week 1 Fri-Sun: China Qualifier (256→4)
- Week 2 Fri-Sat: South China Qualifier (256→2)
- Week 2 Sun + Week 3 Fri: Asia Qualifier (256→2)
- Week 3 Sat-Sun: Main Event Double Elimination

## 5. Compliance
Non-ranked tournament under Valve Limited Game Tournament License. Prize pool ≤ $100,000.
