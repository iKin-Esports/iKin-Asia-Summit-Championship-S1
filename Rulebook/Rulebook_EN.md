# iKin Asia Summit Championship S1 Official Rulebook (v1.0)

---
## 1. Tournament Definition
These rules apply to all stages of the iKin Asia Summit Championship S1.

## 2. Player Eligibility

- Age ≥ 16 years old; CS2 account not VAC banned.

- Each team must have at least 5 and a maximum of 7 players (including substitutes and coaches).

## 3. Special Qualifying Rules

### 3.1 Southern China Region Qualification Verification

Participants must provide proof of household registration or long-term residence in one of the following provinces: Guangdong, Guangxi, Hainan, Fujian, Jiangxi, or Southern Hunan (specifically based on the first two characters of the ID card or residence permit).

### 3.2 Mixed Teams in the Asian Region

- No more than 2 players on a team may hold a Chinese passport/ID card.

- Nationality information for all players must be submitted during registration. The organizer reserves the right to conduct random checks. Violators will be disqualified.

## 4. Tournament Format

- **Qualifiers**: Single-elimination, initial round BO1, midway through the tournament (China Top 8, South Top 16, Asia Top 32) changed to BO3.

- **Main Tournament**: 8 teams, double-elimination.

- First Round Matchups: China 1-4 seeds, South 1-2 seeds, Asia 1-2 seeds, drawn into each region.

- Winners' Bracket Round 1: BO1, the rest BO3, Grand Final: BO5.

## 6. Servers and Anti-Cheat

- China and South regions use mainland servers; Asia region may negotiate the use of Hong Kong/Singapore servers.

- VAC enabled, third-party anti-cheat measures used when necessary.

## 7. Prohibited Behaviors and Penalties

- Cheating and match-fixing will result in immediate disqualification and will be reported to Valve.

- Insulting or discriminatory behavior will be subject to warnings or forfeiture, depending on the severity.
